Metric Interpretation Guide

Mean Absolute Error (MAE)

Measures average prediction error magnitude in grade points. Lower values indicate better predictive accuracy.

Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE)

Penalizes larger outliers more severely than MAE. Lower values indicate fewer large deviations.

Coefficient of Determination (R²)

Represents the percentage of score variance captured by the features. Higher values (closer to 1.0) reflect a better model fit.

Why Compare Both?

Linear Regression provides an interpretable baseline, while SVR (RBF Kernel) can capture non-linear relationships in study patterns and failure counts.
