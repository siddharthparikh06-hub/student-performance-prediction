College Metrics Guide

MAE & RMSE on CGPA

Both models achieve low error (~0.28 to 0.34 CGPA points), demonstrating strong correlation between study hours and CGPA in this dataset.

R² Score (Coefficient of Determination)

An $R^2 \approx 0.975$ indicates that over 97% of the variance in this dataset's CGPA is explained by study hours alone.

Dataset Provenance Warning

This dataset exhibits near-deterministic linearity between study hours and CGPA. Do not extrapolate these high accuracy scores to claim universal prediction of real college students without multi-factor academic data.
